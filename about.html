Our story

A small studio with a big heart.

Julio Visual Arts began in 2013 with one camera, a borrowed lens, and a stubborn belief that the best photographs are the ones where people forget they're being photographed.

How we got here

From one wedding to six hundred stories.

What started as a favour for a friend's wedding turned into a calling. Twelve years later, we've photographed couples in rain and sunshine, welcomed new babies into families, and helped brands tell stories that actually sound like them.

We still shoot every session ourselves. We still edit every frame by hand. And we still get a little lump in our throat when a client sees their gallery for the first time.

What we believe

Three things we never compromise on

Light over gear

A beautiful moment in soft window light will always beat a stiff pose under a flash. We follow the light — and the feeling.

People over poses

We'd rather capture you mid-laugh than perfectly positioned. Real is always more beautiful than perfect.

Craft over speed

Every image is hand-edited. Every album is hand-finished. We'd rather take a little longer and deliver something you'll keep forever.
