Common questions

Good to know

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, 6–12 months is ideal. For portraits and family sessions, 3–4 weeks usually works — but we do keep a few last-minute slots open each month.

Do you travel?

Absolutely. We're based in Riverside but shoot anywhere the light is good. Travel within 100 km is included; beyond that we'll quote a simple travel fee.

How many images will we get?

It depends on the session — a portrait might be 50 edited images, a full wedding 600+. Every gallery is delivered fully edited, high resolution, and ready to print.

Can we meet before booking?

Yes please. We love a quick coffee or video call before anything is confirmed. It's the best way to make sure we're the right fit.

Found the right fit?

Tell us about your date and we'll send availability within one business day.
